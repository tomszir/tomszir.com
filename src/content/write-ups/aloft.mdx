## What it does

Press <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Q</kbd> on any page and a search bar opens over it. Type, press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, and you're there.

- **One search** for open tabs, history, bookmarks and recently closed tabs, with your pinned tabs a keystroke away.
- **Actions and keywords:** type <kbd>&gt;</kbd> to act on tabs, or `yt` and a space to search YouTube.
- **Your look:** light, dark, built-in themes, or your own.

It works in Chrome, Firefox, [Helium](https://helium.computer) and other Chromium browsers.

## Privacy

Aloft makes no network requests and collects nothing; your tabs, history and bookmarks never leave the browser. The [privacy policy](/building/aloft/privacy) has the details, and Aloft is [open source](https://github.com/tomszir/aloft-extension) under the GPL 3.0.
